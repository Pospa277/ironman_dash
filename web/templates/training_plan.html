Progressive training plan to prepare you for race day

Plan Overview

This 12-week training plan progressively builds your endurance across all three disciplines. Each week increases in volume to prepare you for the Ironman distances:

  • Swim: 3.86 km
  • Bike: 180.25 km
  • Run: 42.2 km

Note: Always listen to your body and adjust training as needed. Consult with a coach for personalized training.

Training Tips

🏊 Swimming

  • Focus on technique before speed
  • Practice open water swimming
  • Work on sighting and navigation

🚴 Cycling

  • Build aerobic base with long rides
  • Practice nutrition on the bike
  • Include hill training

🏃 Running

  • Practice brick workouts (bike-to-run)
  • Include easy recovery runs
  • Gradually increase long run distance

💪 Recovery

  • Get 7-9 hours of sleep
  • Include rest days
  • Focus on nutrition and hydration
